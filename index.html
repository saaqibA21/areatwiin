AreaTwin guide

What you are looking at. A digital twin of Royapuram built from 848 surveyed OpenStreetMap streets (143 km). Each vehicle drives the real street graph, obeying one-way streets, narrow-street speed caps and closures.

  • Click a street to inspect it, or close it to forecast the knock-on congestion.
  • Click a vehicle to see its live speed, gap to the leader and route. Use Follow to ride along.
  • Click a place to send visitors to it.
  • Weather floods the low basins, which slows or closes the streets inside them.
